# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, evidence-led, and respectful. Critique the analysis, not the person. Do not publish personal financial data, wallet addresses, or secrets.

## Expected behavior

- Cite sources and dates.
- Separate verified facts from estimates and opinions.
- State uncertainty plainly.
- Respect privacy and local law.
- Accept correction when the evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person’s wallet, identity, or financial data.
- Presenting fabricated data or fake citations.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, signing, or trading.
- Marketing a skill as guaranteed profit.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate this code. Report concerns through the repository’s private security advisory or the maintainers’ contact.
